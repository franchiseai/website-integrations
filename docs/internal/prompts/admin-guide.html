Admin guide: the first sales ​

What you need to know to run the portal for the first sales. The flow: create the brand, upload its media and site, invite the brand, the brand selects, and they may invite their team.

1. Set up the brand ​

  • Create the brand in Admin, Clients.
  • Upload its media in Admin, Media (Brand media ZIP).
  • Upload its site in Admin, Sites (Brand website). The brand sees only the version you mark Show To Brand.
  • Check the prices before you invite anyone:
    • Admin, Kits, Kit Prices sets the prices for all brands.
    • Custom Prices on the brand page sets prices for that brand only.
    • Deal Desk adds discounts.
  • Use Viewing As on the brand page to see the portal as the brand sees it. In that view, Confirm saves nothing and sends no email.

2. Invite the brand ​

  • Go to the brand page, People, Invite Someone. Use Resend Invite if they lose the email.
  • You do not need Convert To Lead. It sends the same invite and also records "Intro Call Held". Use it only when you had the intro call and want the Pipeline to show that.
  • If the invite email is set to Draft For Approval, it waits in Admin, Emails, Drafts until someone approves it.
  • Invite real brands only from live. Staging never emails real clients.

3. Their team ​

  • The brand can invite teammates from its Team page.
  • A teammate with the same email domain as the owner joins at once.
  • A teammate with a different domain waits until you click Approve on the brand page, People.
  • Any teammate who has joined can confirm a selection.

4. When they select ​

  • Nothing is signed or paid in the portal. The brand clicks Confirm My Selection and gets an email.
  • The email promises a document signature request and a payment link, usually within one business day. Keeping that promise is our job.
  • We get a Slack post with the full breakdown (kits, add-ons, amounts, their note), an alert email and a Today item.
  • A new selection replaces and cancels the earlier one. The Slack post shows "Replaces". Always use the latest selection.
  • Send the agreement and the payment link yourself.
  • When the money arrives, go to Admin, Orders and click Mark Paid. It needs a reference and a reason.
  • If the deal falls through, click Cancel in Admin, Orders.

5. Small things ​

  • After a portal release, reload any open portal tab. An open tab keeps running the old version.
  • The brand guide PDFs still say that brands sign in the portal. Ignore that for now.
