## Order of work for a new brand

1. Create the brand in Admin, Clients.
2. Upload the media ZIP in Admin, Media.
3. Upload the website ZIP in Admin, Sites, then click **Show To Brand**.
4. Invite the brand from the brand page, People, **Invite Someone**.
